1. Our Story

Rooted in Pollachi’s Coconut Belt, Exporting to the World

Nilite Exports was founded with a singular commitment: to bridge the gap between South India's richest agricultural hubs and international B2B buyers who demand strict quality consistency.

What started as a direct farm-gate procurement desk in Tamil Nadu’s coconut heartland has grown into an integrated export powerhouse equipped with 7 specialized processing and logistics facilities. Today, we handle multi-tonne monthly shipments of washed low-EC coco peat, fresh produce, and single-origin spices under stringent global compliance standards.
